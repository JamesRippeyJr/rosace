One video. Many clips.

Rosace is a desktop app for creators. It finds the best moments in a long video, cuts them into vertical clips with captions, and helps you schedule them and see how they perform.

How it works

  • Your videos are processed on your own computer. They are not uploaded to Rosace.
  • You review every clip, and nothing is posted without your approval.
  • When you connect YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, Rosace only reads your own account's numbers, such as views, likes and followers, to show them to you.

Rosace is in early development and is currently used privately by its creator.
