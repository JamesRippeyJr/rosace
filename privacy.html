Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Rosace is a desktop app that helps creators turn long videos into short clips, schedule them, and follow how they perform. This policy explains what information Rosace uses and how.

Information Rosace uses

  • Your videos and clips. Rosace processes the videos you add on your own computer to find moments and make clips.
  • Transcripts. To choose the best moments and write captions, Rosace sends the text transcript of a video to Anthropic's Claude AI service. The video itself is not sent.
  • Connected social accounts. If you connect YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, Rosace reads your own account's basic profile (such as your username), your follower count, and your public posts with their counts of views, likes, comments, shares and saves. This access is read-only.

How it is used

Rosace uses this information only to show you your own statistics and to help you create and schedule your own clips. Rosace does not use it for advertising, and does not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

Where it is stored

Your clips, statistics and settings are stored on your own computer. The sign-ins for connected accounts are kept in your computer's secure keychain. Rosace has no server that stores your data.

Services Rosace works with

When you use those features, Rosace communicates with YouTube (Google), Instagram (Meta), TikTok, Anthropic, and the scheduling service you have connected. Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any account at any time in Rosace's Settings. Rosace stops reading it and deletes the saved sign-in.
  • You can also remove Rosace's access from your YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account settings.
  • Deleting Rosace and its data folder removes everything it stored on your computer.

Children

Rosace is not intended for anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
